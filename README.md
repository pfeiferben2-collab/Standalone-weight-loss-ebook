# Transform Your Body - Weight Loss eBook

A professionally designed, print-ready eBook about sustainable weight loss. This beautifully crafted digital book features:

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Design**: Elegant typography with Playfair Display and Lato fonts
- **Print-Ready**: Optimized for DIN A4 PDF output with proper margins and page breaks
- **Comprehensive Content**: 9 chapters covering nutrition, exercise, habits, and psychology
- **Visual Elements**: Infographics, meal plans, tip boxes, and interactive cards
- **Responsive**: Works on all screen sizes from mobile to desktop

## 📖 Chapters

1. **Introduction** - A New Beginning
2. **Understanding Your Body** - How your body stores and burns fat
3. **The Science of Metabolism** - Optimizing your metabolic function
4. **Nutrition Fundamentals** - Macronutrients and meal planning
5. **Building Healthy Habits** - The science of habit formation
6. **Exercise & Movement** - The four pillars of fitness
7. **The Mind-Body Connection** - Emotional eating and mindfulness
8. **Meal Planning Made Easy** - Weekly planning framework
9. **Overcoming Plateaus** - Breaking through weight loss stalls
10. **Maintaining Your Success** - Long-term strategies

## 🖨️ Print as PDF

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Click the "Print / Save as PDF" button
3. In the print dialog, select "Save as PDF"
4. The document is optimized for DIN A4 (210mm × 297mm) format

## 🚀 Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser to view the eBook. No build process or dependencies required!
